Technical playbooks and firewalls alone cannot protect an organization. ODTN Vancouver Module 5 explores how psychological safety, operational burnout, pre-incident legal retainers (MSAs/NDAs), and non-punitive post-mortems determine true cyber resilience.

Psychological Safety & Rotational Shifts

Continuous 18-hour shifts cause cognitive fatigue, tunnel vision, and catastrophic configuration mistakes. Rotational shifts preserve decision-making capability.

Pre-Incident MSAs & Vendor Readiness
