Extended incidents cause tunnel vision and cognitive fatigue. Preserving team composure prevents secondary operational errors and catastrophic misconfigurations.

Vendor & Legal Readiness

Negotiating Master Services Agreements (MSAs) and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) mid-crisis causes lethal delays. Pre-incident contracts are essential.

Therapeutic Post-Mortems

Post-incident reviews must serve as continuous learning frameworks rather than blame sessions, evaluating both technical recovery and human performance.
